A satirical depiction of Donald Trump inspired by Dada graphic design. It's a collage of imagery from the internet showing him as a holy figure in front of a "Golden Throne". The piece was designed for high-quality, edge-to-edge print on 8.5 × 11 inch stock.

Over the imagery are the lyrics to "Ave Imperator", a hymn-like metal song inspired by *Warhammer 40,000*. The song praises the "God-Emperor of Mankind", a fictional figure from that universe, and the comparison has been a long-running joke about Trump and his supporters since he entered politics. The piece is meant to evoke both the near-deification some of his followers show him and his constant posting on social media.

## What I did

Sourced and laid out the imagery, applied the digital effects, and set the type.

## Process

This was an assignment for a History of Graphic Design class: create a piece in the Dada style, with no other guidance beyond what defines Dada itself. Researching the movement on my own, I settled on two key points: Dada is inherently political, and it was topical for its time. Trump is a massively divisive political figure and one of the most topical subjects I could imagine, so this collage came together naturally in my mind. Most of the work was finding exactly the right imagery to drive the idea home.
