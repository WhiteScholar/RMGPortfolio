*Jak & Beans* came from going the extra mile on the final project for my Intro to 2D Animation class (Fall 2025). The assignment was only to storyboard an animated version of *Jack and the Beanstalk* in our own style. I took the extra time to turn those boards into a rough but fully animated short.

## The brief

We were given the scenes the story needed to include:

1. Jack finds a bean.
2. Jack plants the bean, and a huge vine grows from the land to the sky.
3. Jack climbs the vine into the sky.
4. Above the clouds, Jack finds a sleeping giant holding a golden egg.
5. Jack slips the egg from the giant's hand, but before he can get away…
6. The egg cracks, and wings pop out of each side!
7. This wakes the giant.
8. Jack flies down from the clouds holding the egg.
9. Jack finds an axe and chops down the vine.
10. The giant, climbing down after Jack, falls from the vine.
11. The egg fully opens, revealing a golden bird.

## Design

*Jack and the Beanstalk* is an old tale that's often adapted for younger audiences, so I went for a goofy, kid-friendly style and leaned into the "bean" idea. Both Jack and the giant are very bean-shaped, with a little inspiration from the simple character designs of *Among Us*. Jack became **Jak**, and the bird became **Burd**.

The simple style let me storyboard quickly in the last couple of classes of the semester. That left me time to take the boards straight into animation, and the result is a very short, roughly one-minute animated version of the story.

## What I learned

This project really drove home the importance of planning animation sequences up front, and it gave me a much better understanding of the Adobe Animate timeline.

I'm happy with what I made in the time I had. At some point I'd like to revisit it and fully re-animate it, with color and sound, to make it a complete story.
