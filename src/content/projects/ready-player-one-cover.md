A new, minimalist design for the cover of *Ready Player One*, including the inside front and back flaps. Most of the book takes place in a virtual world, so I wanted the cover to evoke it: a **Tron-inspired grid** stretches into the distance and wraps across the whole cover, from flap to flap.

The panels were designed in Illustrator and composited in InDesign.

## What I did

This was an assignment for my *Foundations of Digital Graphic Design* class. It was a solo project: I designed the cover art, typography and layout.

## Process

The brief was to choose a fiction book we found interesting and approach it as if we'd been hired to design a cover that sells it. It was a good exercise in taking an existing work and designing something new that suits it and catches the eye.

I drew on *Tron* and the retrofuturism of the era. Both felt in line with the book's narrative and its own inspirations. For the author bio, I used an illustration of Ernest Cline sourced from the web instead of a photograph, since it fit the design better. My professor disagreed and preferred an actual photo of the author.
