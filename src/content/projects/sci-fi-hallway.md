A short first-person 3D animation through a science-fiction hallway, made in Cinema 4D and rendered with Redshift. It has sound effects (sourced from freesound.org) and backing music I made myself.

## What I did

I modelled the environment assets, set up the camera animation paths, rendered the animation, and composited the final render with audio and music in Adobe Premiere.

## Process

This was a good exercise in the full workflow, from design and layout through animation, rendering and editing, start to finish in Cinema 4D and Premiere. I spent a lot of time going back and forth on lighting tests and asset layouts to build a compelling scene.

The final render took around 25 hours on an RTX 5080.
