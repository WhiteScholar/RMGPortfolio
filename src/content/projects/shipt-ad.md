The assignment for my *Foundations of Digital Graphic Design* class: find a subway or bus stop ad whose design you don't like, and make a new one.

## What I did

I don't live particularly close to the NYC subway or any bus stops, so I went searching online for transit ads to choose from. I settled on a bus stop ad for **Shipt**, a delivery company. I didn't find anything particularly wrong with it, but its image of a man carrying a package along the top of a fence felt closer to what you'd expect from a regular mail carrier like USPS. So I took the delivery somewhere far more outlandish: out into the middle of nowhere.

## Process

The whole ad was designed in Illustrator, and I didn't want any blurry raster images taking away from the finished piece. So after sourcing a clean copy of the Shipt logo, I rebuilt it as a vector. I used desert scenery as a starting point for vector art with a vaguely *Wile E. Coyote and the Road Runner* cartoon style, then paired it with a tagline I wrote to sell the idea.

It was a nice exploration of ad design: considering the product, and how to sell it.
