You're a slime on his way to get his mail, but your dog has chased away the mail-slime! A short game made in Bitsy for *Intro to Game Development* (Spring 2026).

## What I did

Tile and sprite design, the story, level editing, dialogue and scene transitions.

## Working within limits

Bitsy is an incredibly limited engine with a very retro, Atari-like feel. You only get a couple of colors at a time and very simple game logic. That makes it an exercise in creativity, both in crafting the story and in bending the engine to bring that story to life.

To play to my favorite part of the engine, its simple two-tone pixel art, I went with the simplest quest structure: the fetch quest. Our hero, the Slime, needs his mail. As you explore, you find dropped pieces of mail of different kinds while you search for the mail-slime who has the package you've been waiting for.

It was a great exercise in making something compelling and visually interesting from a very small set of features.
