Take on the role of a new employee in a dystopian corporate oversight position. Your job is to make decisions on situations that come to you as "tickets". But HyTek Solutions' management system is run by an advanced AI, **Cortex**, and it's evaluating everything you do. Will you be a good little corporate dog? Balance the job with doing the right thing? Or try to upend the system entirely?

This short demo was built for *Intro to Video Game Development* (Spring 2026). It's a solo project: I designed, coded and wrote the story and systems.

## Design goals

Twine is usually used for visual novels and choose-your-own-adventure fiction. I wanted to see whether that decision-tree structure could feel more like a *game* than interactive fiction. To get there, I wrapped the whole experience in a classic terminal interface, rebuilt from scratch in CSS. That meant brushing up on my CSS considerably.

## The ticket system

The story plays out through a series of corporate-compliance tickets. The events you see and the ending you reach depend on how you do your job. There are several main outcomes:

- **The model employee:** you play the part you were assigned.
- **The conflicted employee:** you show compassion where you can, but never stray too far from your directives.
- **The saboteur:** you quietly work around the system until your actions lead to rebellion.

## What I learned

I worked on this over the first few weeks of the class. It was a good introduction to game design within tight limits: a simple engine, but plenty of room for systems thinking and narrative design.
